How scull treats each drive

Own forecast. Each drive gets its own free-space trend and alerts. Own quarantine. Items never move between drives; undo stays on the same disk. External drives are scanned when connected. Unplugging mid-scan just pauses it. Network and backup drives are skipped: slow to scan and not yours to clean.
